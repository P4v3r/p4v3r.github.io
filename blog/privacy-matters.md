# Privacy Matters in 2026

*Published: April 23, 2026*

---

In an age where every click is tracked, every conversation is analyzed, and every action is logged... **personal privacy isn't paranoia, it's survival**.

## The Reality

- Your email provider reads your emails to show you "relevant ads"
- Your messaging app sells your conversation data
- Your search engine tracks every query you make
- Your "free" cloud storage means they own your files
- Apps request permissions they don't need and nobody asks why

## Why I Built void-sh

Every privacy project I create starts with a simple question: **why does this need to send my data somewhere?**

For void-sh (browser file encryption), the answer is simple: it doesn't. Files should never leave your device. Encryption should happen locally, with keys you control, on hardware you own.

For void-ai, same thing. Why should your conversations with an AI be stored on some company's servers? They train models on your data, they analyze your queries, they build profiles. No thanks.

## What Privacy Gives You

- **Peace of mind** - Noone is watching
- **Freedom of thought** - You can think without being influenced by targeted content
- **Security** - Less data = less attack surface
- **Autonomy** - You control your digital life

## The Hard Truth

Privacy requires effort. It requires you to:
- Pay for services instead of "free"
- Learn new tools and workflows
- Accept some inconvenience
- Question every "free" thing

But it's worth it. Every bit of privacy you claim back is a win.
